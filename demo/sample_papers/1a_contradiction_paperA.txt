Efficient Adapters for Sentence Classification. We study parameter-efficient fine-tuning for text classification. We follow the standard experimental protocol of earlier studies. DeBERTa-v3 achieves 93.4% accuracy on SST-2. On MNLI, DeBERTa-v3 reaches 90.1 accuracy. The ELECTRA-small baseline obtains 81.7 accuracy on QNLI in our runs. Our adapters match full fine-tuning while updating only 2% of the parameters.
